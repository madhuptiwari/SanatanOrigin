# ब्रह्माण्डोत्पत्ति एवं देववंशावली • Cosmic Origin & Divine Pantheon in Sanatan Dharma

An interactive, high-fidelity web application visualizing the **Origin of the Universe and the Complete Divine Genealogies of Gods** according to **Sanatan Dharma (Hinduism)**. The architecture embodies the ancient Vedic motif of the inverted cosmic tree (*ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम्* — *Bhagavad Gita 15.1* & *Katha Upanishad 2.3.1*), with the unmanifest **Parabrahman** at the supreme summit, and all divine manifestations and physical dimensions cascading downwards as branches of parent-to-child descent.

## 🎨 Navigation, Camera Controls & Viewport Geometry

- **Complete Visibility for the Topmost Entry in Every Tradition**:
  - Dynamically calculates the offset below the header and floating filter dock (`getApexScrollY`) so that the apex deity card (`Parabrahman`, `Lord Narayana`, `Sadashiva`, `Nirguna Brahman`) is **100% visible and centered** across all four scriptural modes.
  - Generous top breathing room ensures the Tier 0 Sanskrit guideline (`✦ दिव्य मूलम् • Primordial Supreme Origin`) and golden apex cards are never cut off or occluded.
- **Dynamic Zoom In & Zoom Out (Restored Natural Physics)**:
  - **Mouse Wheel Zoom**: Smooth zoom in (`deltaY < 0`) and zoom out (`deltaY > 0`) towards the mouse cursor point (`0.25x` to `2.5x` scale range).
  - **Touch Pinch-to-Zoom**: Two-finger pinch gesture smoothly scales around the pinch center; single finger pans.
  - **Dock Controls**: `🔍+` (Zoom In) and `🔍−` (Zoom Out) centered on the screen; `⌂` (Reset to Apex) smoothly glides back to the root at optimal scale.
- **Sacred Cosmic Tier Guidelines**:
  - Subtle golden guidelines with Sanskrit inscriptions behind the branches (`✦ दिव्य मूलम् • Primordial Supreme Origin`, `✦ आदि सृष्टि • Primary Manifestations & Trideva`, `✦ देववंशावली • Celestial Offspring & Avataras`, `✦ लोकपाल • Cosmic Guardians`).
- **Smart Progressive Hierarchy**:
  - On load, Root and Level 1 primary branches appear framed on the screen.
  - Sub-branches display glowing child count pills (e.g. `[ 3 Child Gods ▸ ]`).
  - Clicking a pill expands that branch and auto-refits the viewport so the newly opened nodes remain inside the device screen.
  - Dock button `[ 🌳 / 📁 ]` toggles between Expand All and Compact Screen View.
- **3-Zone Header Bar**:
  - Brand on Left, Tradition Segmented Tabs in Center, Search + Audio + Tour on Right.
- **Floating Sub-Filter Dock (`#floating-filter-dock`)**:
  - Centered dock beneath header for quick category jumps.
